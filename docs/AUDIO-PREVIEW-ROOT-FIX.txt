Burnout 3 - pause Audio preview interpolation correction (2026-10-14)

STATUS: USER PLAYTESTING CONFIRMS THE PREVIEW STILL SOUNDS BAD.
The volume measurements below demonstrate a narrower interpolation improvement,
not a solution to the audible problem. The local installation has been reverted
to edition 1's muted pause-preview workaround pending further investigation.

Diagnosis:
RWA's native current-volume polling occurs roughly every 33 ms in pause Audio.
Its linear volume ramps reach the requested level in about 10 ms. With delayed
polling, the ramps run beyond their intended endpoint and the next update
reverses them. Tracing showed repeated near-zero/full-scale oscillation.
Disabling reverb did not resolve it. The front-end preview has stable levels.

Correction:
The native stereo/surround preview functions run unmodified again; suppression
is removed. While pause Audio is open, a port-specific resolver reads the exact
left/right targets from the native RWA interpolation call sites. An opt-in SPU2
sweep endpoint keeps their rates but settles at those targets until the next
native poll. Front-end and race sweeps retain their ordinary hardware behavior.
This compensates for the port's coarse polling; it does not make the entire
IOP scheduler cycle-accurate.

Validation:
- Native host tests passed, including increasing, decreasing and signed sweep
  endpoints, native register readback, and clearing an endpoint on a new sweep.
- Full race/pause/Audio/engine-volume/resume fixture reached frame 19900.
- Before-menu PCM (first 300 seconds) was byte-identical to the baseline.
- Resumed-race PCM (325-330 seconds) was byte-identical to the previous build.
- Complete front-end Settings -> Audio fixture PCM was byte-identical.
- Preview channels 17/18 no longer oscillated: steady left/right levels were
  20690/20804, changing to 19514/19624 when Effects Volume was lowered, then
  returning to 20690/20804 when raised. These match the native requested targets.
- Preview interval 303-310 seconds stayed audible: RMS 11433.50 versus the
  muted workaround's 163.33. Final-output clipped samples fell from 647 to 13
  out of 672000 samples; small remaining loud transients are not claimed fixed.
- Own engine volume could still be changed and saved in the native Audio menu.

Local replay tests use a private configuration and no personal memory card.
User listening confirmation is still useful; other tracks/audio-output modes
have not been exhaustively playtested.

Follow-up investigation after the failed user listening test (2026-10-14):
- Pause Audio looks up the boostloop and fire loops in loaded race banks.
- Front-end Audio uses a different dedicated left/right preview from fe.awd.
- The two paused-preview ADPCM buffers captured in SPU RAM match their full
  source buffers on the owned ISO byte for byte (34,448 and 14,864 bytes).
  No overwritten or damaged sample bytes were found in this test.
- Redirecting playback to the front-end routine alone is silent: its bank
  is absent during a race. Loading that bank alongside race audio did not
  return a usable bank, including a bounded retry experiment. Rejected.
- These findings do not establish the cause of the objectionable sound.
  The main source and installed executable retain edition 1's muted pause
  preview workaround. No further candidate has been installed or released.
